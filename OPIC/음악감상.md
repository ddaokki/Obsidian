##### MBC
Main Idea: 질문 확인, 너에게 소개해줄게
Body: 
- 좋아하는 장르
	- 나 다 좋아하는데? -> 그중에서 한가지를 고르자면..
	- 장르: J-POP
		- 이유1: J-POP 특유의 신나는 멜로디 - 날 항상 흥겹게 함
		- 이유2: 일본어도 같이 배울 수 있어서 좋음
	- 좋아하는 가수: YOASOBI
		- 유명한 일본 2인조 유닛
		- 굉장히 많은 히트곡 보유 : 밤을 달리다, IDOL , UNDEAD..
		- 그중 최애곡은? 밤을 달리다 - 빌보드 재팬 연간 1위곡, 가사의 의미와 문학성이 정말 좋아서 계속 듣게됨
Conclusion: 어쨌든, 나는 요아소비를 좋아해. 넌 어때?

##### Script
Alright, Ava. Um, you mean my favorite kind of music and musician. right? Well, I love this question as a music lover.

Well, I actually love all kind of music like hip-hop, dance, jazz- you know, But.. um.. If i had to pick just one, I'd always choose J-POP. You know what? It's own unique melody totally pumps me up. I mean, it makes me so happy. Plus, I can learn japanese so easily by just listening J-POPs. By listening to J-pop songs, I can pick up pronunciation and expressions without even studying. It's very efficient, huh? (얼마나 효율적이야?)

Anyway, speaking of my favorite musician, well.. my all-time favorite singer is YOASOBI. you know, the really famous Japanese two-member music unit, right? Well, they have so many hits like Racing into the Night, IDOL, Undead..you know. These days, I'm hooked in 'Racing into the Night'.. it ranked number one on the Billboard Japan, so wonderful right? 

What I like most about this song is the meaning of the lyrics. The lyrics are so deep and literary that sometimes it feels like I’m reading a poem. you know what i mean, right? Because of that, I never get tired of listening to it.

So yeah, what I'm saying is, I'm all about YOASOBI! And.. what about you? Who's your favorite singer?

### 음악 감상하기 3
#### 선호 음악 장르의 변천사 
##### MBC
Main idea: 나의 음악 장르 선호는 좀 변한 편이야.
Body: 어릴때 - R&B 를 좋아하였음. 고등학교 시절에 매일 R&B 장르의 노래를 들었고, 친구들과 노래방에 가서 부르곤 했었음. 하지만 그렇게 잘 부르지는 못했음.

어쨌든, R&B 장르는 조금 루즈한 감이 없잖아 있음. R&B 장르의 진심어린 가사와 소울은 날 감동시키지만...너무 느린 템포는 날 지루하게 만들긴해
그리고 노래들이 서로 좀 비슷한 감도 없잖아 있어 안그래?

요즘 - 좀 많이 다른 취향의 노래를 들음. J-POP.
이건 나를 항상 신나게 만들고 긍정적인 영향을 줘.
Conclusion: 내 요지는.. 내 음악 장르 선호도는 완전히 뒤바뀌었어.

Okay...Ava..I think my taste in music has changed a lot.
Well..Back in the day.. I mean, when I was in high school..I used to listen to R&B music. It's not common choose..right? I know.. my friends almost like K-POP, or Hip hop...exciting..energetic music.. you know.. But Actually, I loved R&B melodies, so I would sing their songs by myself sometimes, but honestly, I wasn’t a very good singer right?

So.. Anyway R&B music is mostly slow and peaceful, right? so it really calms me down when I'm stressed or upset about something. you know what I mean. But on the other hand, , It's too slow and sometimes makes me bored. you know, And honestly, a lot of R&B songs kind of sound similar to each other. right?

So..these days, I'm into totally different music, J-POP. First of all, it gets me excited and hyped. It gives me such positive vibes, and I can actually relieve my stress from work and studying when I listen to it.

So, yeah. my point is.. my taste in music has completely changed.
